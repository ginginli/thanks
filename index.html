    Q FAQ

    How much turkey do I need per person?

    Plan on 1.5 lbs of whole turkey per adult; children under 12 count as half, teens and athletes count as 1.5. For 8 adults and 2 kids, that's a 17–18 lb bird. Pick a leftover mode: Some +30% buffers the turkey, potatoes and dinner rolls (the most fought-over leftovers), while Heavy +50% scales everything for to-go plates and big eaters.

    When should I move my turkey to the fridge to thaw?

    Fridge thawing takes about one day per 4 lbs, so a 17.6 lb turkey needs roughly 4.4 days, per the USDA safe thawing guide. HostCalc Pro back-calculates the exact date and time from your dinner time.

    How long does a turkey take to cook?

    At 325 °F / 165 °C, we follow the USDA weight timetable — roughly 13 minutes per pound for large birds (20+ lb) up to 22 minutes per pound for small ones — then rest the bird for 45 minutes after pulling it at 165 °F internal temperature, the safe minimum recommended by the USDA FSIS.

    Do children eat as much as adults?

    No — each child under 12 is counted as half an adult across the grocery list, except for dinner rolls (one per child) and wine (adults only).

    How much do teenagers eat at Thanksgiving?

    Count teens and athletes as big eaters — about 1.5 adults' worth of food each. Use the Big Eaters field (not the regular Adults box) so the food scales up without inflating the wine count.

    Is my data stored anywhere?

    Not on any server. HostCalc Pro is a single static page — every calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. The only thing saved is your latest plan, stored on your own device (browser localStorage) so you can reuse it next year or hand it over via the share link. Clearing your browser data removes it.

    What portion sizes does the calculator use per person?

    Per equivalent adult (children under 12 count as half, big eaters like teens and athletes count as 1.5): 1.5 lb whole turkey, 0.25 lb (4 oz) stuffing, 0.33 lb (about 150 g) potatoes, 3.5 oz veggie sides, 2 fl oz cranberry sauce, 2 oz cheese, 7 pieces of appetizers, and one 9-inch pie per 6 people. Dinner rolls are 2 per adult (big eaters included) and 1 per child. Wine is 0.71 liters (about 24 fl oz) per regular adult only — big eaters and children don't add wine. Leftover modes: Some adds +30% to the turkey, potatoes and rolls; Heavy adds +50% to everything.

    How does the reverse schedule work?

    We subtract backwards from your dinner time: first pull the bird 45 minutes early to rest; before that, roast at 325 °F following the USDA weight timetable (about 13 minutes per pound for a 20+ lb bird, up to 22 minutes per pound for small birds); and before that, fridge-thaw at one day per 4 lbs. The result is the exact date and time to move your turkey into the fridge.

    How long does it take to thaw a turkey in cold water?

    About 30 minutes per pound in cold water, changing the water every 30 minutes — a 15 lb bird takes roughly 7.5 hours. The fridge method (one day per 4 lbs) is safer and needs no babysitting, which is why HostCalc Pro schedules it by default.
